# Cost model

Private-beta planning assumes fewer than 100 monthly users, under 250 MB application data, bounded daily provider imports, and modest CI frequency. Alert at the registry threshold, forecast at 50/70/90 percent of quota, and require owner approval before paid overages.

Track MAU, database/storage size, egress, Auth email volume, provider credits, CI minutes/artifacts, telemetry events, monitor checks, and archive operations. A zero-dollar infrastructure plan does not include market-data licensing, legal review, labor, backups, incident response, or paid growth.
