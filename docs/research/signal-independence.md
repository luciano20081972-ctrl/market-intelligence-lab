# Signal Independence

Signal Independence compares a candidate with the versioned `conventional-baseline-v1`: momentum, size, valuation, revenue growth, profitability, volatility, sector, and market exposure.

The analysis reports Pearson and Spearman overlap, residual/partial contribution, candidate and baseline out-of-sample rank information coefficients, incremental contribution, redundancy, and the formula components of Independent Information Score v1. Predictive strength and unique contribution are displayed separately. Independent does not mean causal or profitable.
