# Skeptic engine

The deterministic skeptic maps mechanical safeguards—temporal warnings, entity ambiguity, low coverage, redundancy, contradictions, regime dependence, fragility, negative controls, and weak mechanisms—to structured challenges. Critical open challenges block new paper eligibility; high challenges require resolution or an explicit accepted-risk policy. Historical promotions are never rewritten.

Optional reasoning providers may propose structured candidates, but are disabled by default and cannot alter evidence, results, gates, code, SQL, shell state, or Git.
