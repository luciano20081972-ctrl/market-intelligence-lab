# Hypothesis Factory

The factory accepts a Level-4 v0.9 research candidate, loads its driver profile, evidence-backed paths, relevance decisions, and feature snapshot, then creates distinct deterministic mechanisms, deduplicates semantic checksums, verifies data, writes declarative feature specifications, checks budgets, and schedules experiments. Default generation is at most 10 hypotheses per company per cycle and the configured hard limit is 25. Runtime reasoning is optional; an idea never becomes a signal without implementation and validation.

The semiconductor fixture examines regional electricity exposure, the airline fixture fuel/weather operations, and the agriculture fixture water/weather stress. The agriculture candidate is deliberately rejected for absent OOS persistence, multiple-testing failure, and no incremental information.
