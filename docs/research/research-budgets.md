# Research budgets

Versioned per-level budgets bound population, API requests, downloaded and stored bytes, estimated CPU seconds, future token allowance, refresh cadence, and concurrent jobs. Cost classes are `free`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, and `premium`; monetary estimates are optional and absent where pricing is not defensible. Ranking happens before execution: if 500 companies request a level capped at 100, the first 100 are accepted reproducibly and 400 are deferred.
