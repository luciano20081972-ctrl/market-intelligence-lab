# Divergence Engine

A divergence definition declares domains, normalization, thresholds, minimum persistence, evidence requirements, and a version. Detection stores the normalized domain values, disagreeing directions, pairwise spreads, magnitude, persistence, evidence quality, provenance, and simulation-eligible time in an immutable event.

Historical analogues carry sample-size warnings. A detected event may become a research candidate, but is never automatically eligible for paper or real trading. Divergent does not mean mispriced.
