# Negative controls and leakage attacks

Each reference pipeline retains shuffled-factor, deterministic-noise, unrelated-sector, and controlled-temporal-corruption controls. Persistent control power marks the methodology invalid. The attack suite rejects future ALFRED vintages, SEC disclosures, graph relationships, feature revisions, universe membership, future normalization, publication-time leakage, target/label leakage, and post-event identifiers.

Controls and attacks are validation artifacts, not successful factors; all outcomes are saved to prevent selective reporting.
